# KAI — Roadmap

## Current Phase

**Phase 1 — Engineering Proof**

Close critical semantics and build one complete vertical slice:

Intent → Contract → Planning / KAI-IR → Policy → Approval → Identity → Action → Evidence → Evaluation → Fulfillment → Recovery

**Current vertical slice candidate:** Schedule-a-Meeting / Calendar

## Phase 2 — Product Proof

Convert the vertical slice into a real, usable demo. Focus on demonstrating the complete experience end-to-end (not a large dashboard). Goal is to prove the full experience is usable by a real user.

### Initial product-proof signal — conversation-provided

This is an initial qualitative signal from answers provided in a conversation, not
an independently conducted user interview or market validation. The participant's
identity and interview context were not independently documented.

- Stated usefulness: **4/5**.
- Stated confidence in handling duplicate-execution risk: **4/5**.
- Understanding of the plan before approval: **yes, according to the answers**.
- Understanding of an ambiguous write outcome: **yes, according to the answers**.
- Largest stated blocker: the absence of a simple approval and follow-up interface.
- Conclusion: terminal and API workflows are suitable for technical evaluation,
  but everyday use needs an easier interface.

**Product direction to evaluate:** continue the Calendar slice and assess a small
approval-and-follow-up interface as the next usability blocker. This signal does
not establish market demand, represent an independently verified user trial, or
authorize a full interface or changes to the Runtime execution and approval paths.

## Phase 3 — Market Validation
After completing Product Proof:
- 10–20 interviews with potential users
- 3–5 real trials
- Attempt to secure first paid customer

Validation criterion: "This is a real problem—I want to try it, or I'm willing to pay for it."

## Phase 4 — Expansion after traction

**Not started in current phase.** After demonstrated technical and commercial evidence, expansion may include:
- SDK/API
- Additional integrations
- Multi-Agent
- Computer Use
- Enterprise deployment
- Control Plane
- Platform capabilities

> No expansion without demonstrated technical and commercial evidence. Expansion only after traction.
